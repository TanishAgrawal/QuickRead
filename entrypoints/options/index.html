A little room to focus.

Highlight text on a webpage, activate QuickRead, then press Start. Choose Word or a smoothly scrolling Line in the reader, with the page still within reach.

Set your pace

Start comfortably. Change speed from the reader at any time.

02

Leave a little breathing room

Extra time after each word. If pauses overlap, the longest wins.

At 300 WPM, a word lasts 200 ms before extra pauses. Paragraph timing depends on the line breaks supplied by the selected webpage.

03

Keep the controls close

Focus a shortcut field and press a key or combination. Tab moves to the next field.

Jumping preserves paused or playing state. Controls leave typing in page fields alone. Site or browser shortcuts may take priority.

Reading stays on this device.

QuickRead saves preferences and panel position locally. Selected text stays in memory for the reading session. No accounts, analytics, or text uploads.

Works with selectable webpage text. Chrome’s built-in PDF viewer, browser pages, and the Chrome Web Store are unsupported in this version. Cross-origin frames and canvas-based editors may not expose selection. Local HTML files require “Allow access to file URLs” in extension details.

The panel starts at the top right and remembers where you drag it. Resize from its bottom-right corner. Reset clears the session and loads your next highlight, ready for Start. Switching tabs pauses playback.
